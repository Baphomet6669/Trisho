Backlinks A-Z — Easy Learning Guide

1. Backlink kya hota hai?

Simple language mein:

Backlink = Jab kisi doosri website se tumhari website par link aata hai.

Example:

<a href="https://example.com">
  Visit Example
</a>

Agar "abc.com" ke page par ye link laga hai aur visitor "example.com" par ja sakta hai, to "example.com" ko backlink mila.

---

2. Backlink ka Simple Example

Maan lo tumhari website hai:

Parvati Singh Foods

Aur ek food blogger ne article likha:

«"Mumbai ke best homemade food creators mein Parvati Singh Foods bhi ek interesting food brand hai."»

Aur usne "Parvati Singh Foods" par tumhari website ka link laga diya.

To:

Food Blogger Website → Parvati Singh Foods

Ye backlink hai.

---

3. Contextual Backlink

Link article ke actual content ke andar naturally placed ho.

Example:

<p>
  Agar aapko healthy Indian recipes pasand hain,
  <a href="https://example.com">
    Parvati Singh Foods
  </a>
  ko follow kar sakte hain.
</p>

Easy meaning:

Article ke paragraph ke andar link = Contextual backlink

---

4. Editorial Backlink

Website owner/editor ne naturally tumhare content ko useful samajhkar link diya.

Example:

<p>
  Healthy breakfast ke liye
  <a href="https://example.com">
    Parvati Singh Foods
  </a>
  ki recipes useful ho sakti hain.
</p>

Ye generally strong type ka backlink hota hai.

---

5. Guest Post Backlink

Tum kisi doosri website ke liye article likhte ho.

Example:

<h2>10 Healthy Indian Breakfast Ideas</h2>

<p>
  Healthy breakfast ideas ke liye
  <a href="https://example.com">
    Parvati Singh Foods
  </a>
  dekhein.
</p>

Website tumhara article publish karti hai → tumhe backlink milta hai.

---

6. Niche Edit / Link Insertion

Article pehle se published hai.

Baad mein relevant paragraph mein tumhara link add hota hai.

Example:

<p>
  Homemade recipes ke liye
  <a href="https://example.com">
    Parvati Singh Foods
  </a>
  useful resource hai.
</p>

Old article + new relevant link = Niche Edit

⭐ Sabse Easy Formula

Backlink ko identify karne ke liye ye 4 questions pucho:

Question 1 — Link kahan se aa raha hai?

Blog
News
Directory
Forum
Social
Podcast
Profile
Resource Page

Question 2 — Link kahan placed hai?

Content
Author Bio
Sidebar
Footer
Profile
Image

Question 3 — Link attribute kya hai?

Normal/Follow
Nofollow
Sponsored
UGC

Question 4 — Link kis page par ja raha hai?

Homepage
Blog
Category
Product
Specific Landing Page

---

🏆 Backlink Quality Formula

Simple way mein:

Relevant Website
        +
Trusted Website
        +
Useful Content
        +
Natural Placement
        +
Relevant Anchor
        +
Real Audience
        =
Strong Backlink

❌ Ye mat sochna:

1000 backlinks = Guaranteed Ranking

Instead:

10 Relevant + High-Quality Links
             >
1000 Spammy Links

🔥 Final Rule

Backlink building ka real goal:

«"Google ko manipulate karne ke liye links banana nahi, balki doosri trusted websites par apne brand/content ke genuine references earn karna."»

Aur sabse important:

Relevance > Quantity
Quality > Quantity
Natural > Manipulative
Editorial > Spam
Useful Content > Random Links
