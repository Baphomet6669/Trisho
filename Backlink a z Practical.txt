Bilkul bhai. Ab theory repeat nahi karunga. Neeche main isi complete backlink chapter ka practical roadmap de raha hoon — yani 73 points ko real life mein kaise identify, create/earn, check, analyse aur manage karna hai. Kuch bhi major skip nahi karunga.

Ek important correction pehle: har backlink ko manually “create” nahi kiya ja sakta. Editorial, news, research, image-credit jaise links ko normally earn kiya jata hai. Aur PBN/spam/bulk manipulative links ko practical strategy ke taur par use nahi karna hai.

1. Editorial Backlink

Practical kya karna hai?

Useful content banao jise doosre bloggers naturally reference kar sakein.

Example:

Tum article publish karo:

> 25 Healthy Indian Breakfast Recipes



Food blogger apne article mein likhta hai:

> "For more healthy Indian breakfast ideas, see Parvati Singh Foods."



Aur link deta hai.

<a href="https://yourwebsite.com/healthy-breakfast">
Parvati Singh Foods
</a>

Tumhara practical work:

Research
↓
Excellent article
↓
Publish
↓
Promote
↓
People discover it
↓
Natural mentions
↓
Backlinks


---

2. Guest Post Backlink

Practical process

Google par relevant websites find karo:

"write for us" food
"guest post" recipes
"submit article" food
"contribute" cooking

Website check karo.

Phir webmaster ko pitch:

I would like to contribute an original article about
healthy Indian breakfast recipes for your readers.

Article genuinely useful ho.

Relevant context mein link:

<a href="https://yourwebsite.com/healthy-breakfast">
healthy breakfast recipes
</a>

Avoid

100 websites ko same spun article bhejna ❌

3. Niche Edit / Link Insertion

Existing article find karo.

Example:

> "Best Healthy Breakfast Ideas"

Agar webmaster agree karta hai:

<p>
For more ideas, see
<a href="https://yourwebsite.com/healthy-breakfast">
this healthy breakfast guide
</a>.
</p>

If you find it genuinely relevant, feel free to

[Name]
