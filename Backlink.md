Bilkul bhai. Backlinks ke A to Z types ko main categories ke saath bata raha hoon, taaki koi major type miss na ho.

🔗 A to Z Backlinks Types

1. Editorial Backlink

Kisi article/content mein naturally tumhari website ka link mention hona.

Example: Kisi food blogger ne article likha aur usme Parvati Singh Foods ko reference kar diya.


---

2. Guest Post Backlink

Kisi doosri website par article publish karke us article/profile/author bio se backlink lena.


---

3. Niche Edit / Link Insertion

Already-published article ke andar naturally relevant jagah par tumhara link add hona.


---

4. Contextual Backlink

Article ke relevant paragraph/content ke andar placed link.

Usually: High-value backlink type.


---

5. Resource Page Backlink

Kisi website ki "Useful Resources", "Recommended Websites", "Helpful Links" page par link milna.


---

6. Directory Backlink

Online directories/business directories mein website listing create karke link lena.


---

7. Local Citation Backlink

Business ka Name + Address + Phone + Website kisi local/business platform par list hona.

Example:

Business directories

Local listings

Regional portals



---

8. Social Profile Backlink

Social media profile ke website/bio section mein website link.

Example:

Facebook

Instagram

LinkedIn

Pinterest

YouTube


Note: Mostly nofollow/ugc type signals ho sakte hain, but branding/discovery ke liye useful hain.


---

9. Social Bookmarking Backlink

Bookmarking platforms par webpage submit karna.


---

10. Forum Backlink

Relevant forum/community discussion mein genuinely useful answer ke context mein website link.

Spam nahi karna.


---

11. Blog Comment Backlink

Blog ke comments mein website link.

Ye historically popular tha, but low-quality/spam comments generally useful nahi hote.


---

12. Q&A Backlink

Question-answer websites par helpful answer ke saath relevant link.

Example:

Quora-type platforms

Industry Q&A communities



---

13. Web 2.0 Backlink

Third-party publishing platforms par content publish karke backlink.

Example:

Medium

Blogger

WordPress.com-type platforms



---

14. Blog Backlink

Kisi blog/article se tumhari website ko link milna.

Iske andar editorial, guest post, contextual etc. aa sakte hain.


---

15. News/PR Backlink

News publication, press release, online magazine ya media article se backlink.


---

16. Digital PR Backlink

Journalists/publications ko useful information, data, expert commentary etc. provide karke editorial mentions/backlinks earn karna.


---

17. HARO/Journalist Request Backlink

Journalist ke query/request ka expert response dena aur publication mein mention/link milna.

Note: Original HARO service ka status/platform change ho chuka hai, isliye concept ko broader journalist-request platforms ke sense mein samjho.


---

18. Interview Backlink

Online interview/podcast/interview article mein tumhara brand mention + website link.


---

19. Podcast Backlink

Podcast episode/show notes/guest profile se website backlink.


---

20. Video Backlink

YouTube/video platform description, profile, associated webpage etc. se link.


---

21. Image Backlink

Kisi website par tumhari original image use hone par source/credit link milna.


---

22. Infographic Backlink

Infographic publish karna aur other websites ka us infographic ko reference/embed karna.


---

23. Original Research Backlink

Original research/data/statistics publish karna jise other websites cite karein.

Example:
"2026 Indian Homemade Food Trends Report"


---

24. Statistics/Data Backlink

Useful statistics/data source provide karna, jise articles reference karein.


---

25. Case Study Backlink

Detailed case study publish karna aur other websites ka usse reference karna.


---

26. Testimonials Backlink

Kisi company/product/service ka genuine testimonial dena aur company website par testimonial ke saath tumhara link milna.


---

27. Sponsor Backlink

Legitimate event/community/project sponsorship ke context mein website mention/link.


---

28. Partner Backlink

Business partners/suppliers/distributors/associations ki websites se legitimate links.


---

29. Association Backlink

Industry association, professional organization ya membership page se backlink.


---

30. Chamber of Commerce Backlink

Local chamber/business organization ki member directory/profile se backlink.


---

31. Educational / .edu Backlink

Educational institution ki website se link.

Important: .edu extension automatically backlink ko powerful nahi banata. Relevance + authority + editorial context important hai.


---

32. Government / .gov Backlink

Government website se backlink.

Again, .gov automatically magic SEO boost nahi deta.


---

33. Wikipedia-type Reference Backlink

Wikipedia ya similar knowledge resource mein legitimate citation/reference.

Very difficult + rules strict. Self-promotion/spam nahi.


---

34. Resource / Link List Backlink

"Khaas websites", "Best blogs", "Useful tools", "Recommended resources" type list mein inclusion.


---

35. Roundup Backlink

"Top 20 Food Blogs", "Best Indian Recipes Websites" jaise roundup article mein mention.


---

36. Listicle Backlink

List-based article mein brand/website inclusion.


---

37. Broken Link Building Backlink

Kisi website ka broken/outdated link identify karke webmaster ko relevant replacement resource suggest karna.


---

38. Link Reclamation

Tumhara brand/content already mention hai but link missing/broken hai → webmaster se link add/fix karwana.


---

39. Unlinked Brand Mention

Website par tumhara brand mention hai but hyperlink nahi hai.

Us mention ko backlink mein convert karna.


---

40. Competitor Backlink Gap

Competitors ko jo backlinks mil rahe hain unmein se relevant opportunities identify karna aur legitimate way se apne liye earn karna.


---

41. Skyscraper Backlink

Existing popular content se better/more useful content create karke un sites ko outreach karna jo old resource ko link kar rahi hain.


---

42. Ego Bait Backlink

Expert/creator/business ko article mein genuinely feature karna → unko notify karna → possible social share/link.


---

43. Expert Roundup Backlink

Multiple experts se contributions lekar article publish karna; participants potentially article share/link karte hain.


---

44. Community Backlink

Relevant online community se contextual link/mention.


---

45. Profile Backlink

Website/profile page ke bio/profile section mein website URL.


---

46. Author Bio Backlink

Guest article ya publication ke author profile/bio se backlink.


---

47. Footer Backlink

Website ke footer mein link.

Usually sitewide links hote hain; SEO ke liye aggressively build karna recommended nahi.


---

48. Sidebar Backlink

Website sidebar mein link.

Same issue: sitewide/sidebar links ko manipulate nahi karna chahiye.


---

49. Blogroll Backlink

Blog ke permanent "Recommended Blogs/Links" section se backlink.


---

50. Sitewide Backlink

Ek website ke bahut saare pages par same backlink.

Example: footer/sidebar.


---

51. Reciprocal Backlink

Website A → Website B
Website B → Website A

Natural relationship mein ho sakta hai, but excessive/large-scale link exchange risky ho sakta hai.


---

52. Three-Way / ABC Link

A → B
B → C
C → A

Link-exchange schemes mein use hota hai; manipulation ke liye use karna risky hai.


---

53. PBN Backlink

Private Blog Network ki websites se backlinks.

High-risk SEO tactic. Search-engine guidelines violate karne wali manipulative link schemes mein aa sakta hai.


---

54. Paid Backlink

Paise dekar backlink lena.

Paid links agar ranking manipulate karne ke liye hain, to proper sponsored treatment/disclosure zaroori ho sakta hai.


---

55. Sponsored Backlink

Sponsored content/ad placement se link.

Link ko appropriately rel="sponsored" mark kiya ja sakta hai.


---

56. Affiliate Backlink

Affiliate relationship ke through link.

Affiliate links generally appropriately marked hote hain, e.g. rel="sponsored".


---

57. UGC Backlink

User-generated content se backlink.

rel="ugc" attribute relevant hai.


---

58. Nofollow Backlink

Link ke saath:

rel="nofollow"

attribute.


---

59. Dofollow / Follow Link

Normal crawlable hyperlink jisme nofollow, sponsored, etc. directive nahi hai.

Actually "dofollow" koi HTML attribute nahi hai—SEO industry ka informal term hai.


---

60. Sponsored Link

Paid/sponsored placement ke context ka link.

rel="sponsored"


---

61. UGC Link

User-generated content ka link.

rel="ugc"


---

62. Image-Embedded Link

Image ko clickable banakar website link dena.


---

63. Anchor Text Backlink

Link ke visible clickable words ko anchor text kehte hain.

Example:

> Healthy Indian Recipes




---

64. Exact-Match Anchor Backlink

Target keyword exactly anchor text mein.

Example:

> Healthy Breakfast Recipe




---

65. Partial-Match Anchor Backlink

Keyword + additional words.

Example:

> easy healthy breakfast recipes




---

66. Branded Anchor Backlink

Brand name anchor.

Example:

> Parvati Singh Foods




---

67. Naked URL Backlink

Anchor mein actual URL.

Example:

> parvatisinghfoods.com




---

68. Generic Anchor Backlink

Generic words:

> Click here
Visit website
Learn more




---

69. Natural Anchor Backlink

Publisher naturally apne words mein link karta hai.


---

70. Deep Link

Homepage ke instead website ke specific internal page ko backlink.

Example:

/healthy-breakfast-recipe


---

71. Homepage Backlink

Direct homepage ko backlink.


---

72. Category/Page Backlink

Specific category, service, product, recipe etc. page ko backlink.


---

73. Internal Backlink

Same website ke andar ek page se doosre page ko link.

Technically ye external backlink nahi hai; ye internal link hai. SEO strategy mein isko separate category samjho.


---

⭐ Sabse important classification

Backlinks ko practically 4 dimensions mein samjho:

1. Source/type

Editorial

Guest post

Directory

Forum

Q&A

PR

Resource

Podcast

Interview

Profile

etc.


2. Placement

Contextual

Author bio

Sidebar

Footer

Image

Profile

Resource page


3. Link attribute

Follow

Nofollow

Sponsored

UGC


4. Destination

Homepage

Deep page

Category

Product

Blog/article


🏆 Quality ke perspective se generally priority

Editorial + Relevant + Contextual + Trusted website
⬆️ Highest priority

Then:

Digital PR / Original Research / Resource links / Relevant guest contributions / Industry mentions

Then:

Relevant directories / citations / profiles / communities

Aur sabse last mein:

Random comments, mass forum links, automated links, PBNs, spammy directories, bulk link packages

Important: Backlinks mein goal "1000 links banana" nahi hai. Goal hai relevant, trustworthy, editorially earned links banana. Google ke liye manipulative link building उल्टा problem create kar sakti hai.
